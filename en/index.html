NIMA
A place just for the two of you

NIMA connects exactly two people, and no one else. Share Talk, plans, photos and Wishes just between the two of you. Everything is encrypted on your phone before it is sent, so no one but the two of you can read it.

Questions

How do we connect?

On the first screen, both of you choose “Connect with a number”. One of you taps “Show a number”, the other taps “Enter a number” and types the 6 digits. Finally, check that the same numbers appear on both screens. You can also connect by bringing your phones close together.

Can anyone else join?

No. Only the two people who connected can enter a space. No one can be added later.

The numbers we compared were different

Someone may be trying to get in between you. Stop, and connect again from the beginning. After connecting, you can compare the same numbers any time in Menu → “Privacy & protection”.

I'm changing phones

On your old phone, open the menu and choose “Move to a new phone”. On the new phone, choose “Move from your previous phone” on the first screen and enter the number. Everything stays as it is.

I lost or broke my phone

Install NIMA on your new phone and open “Move from your previous phone” on the first screen. Ask your partner to choose “Welcome your partner's new phone” in their menu, and enter the number it shows. For safety, there is a 24-hour wait before the new phone can enter. During that time, either of you can cancel it.

Are there notifications?

No. To avoid rushing each other, NIMA doesn't use notifications. New messages are waiting for you when you open NIMA.

How much does it cost?

If you're new, the first month is free. After that, it is a small monthly or yearly subscription, one per person. The price is shown in the app before you subscribe. It renews automatically. You can cancel any time in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

If I stop subscribing, is everything erased?

No. Your space and everything in it stays, and you can still look at it. You just can't send or add anything new. If you subscribe again, you can pick up where you left off.

Do I have to pay again on a new phone?

No. With the same Apple Account, tap “Restore purchases” on the subscription screen.

How do we stop using NIMA?

Choose “End your space” in the menu. Talk, plans, photos and Wishes are all erased. This can't be undone.

Can anyone else see what we share?

No. Everything is encrypted with a key that exists only on your two phones. Neither the server nor the person who made NIMA can read it. See the Privacy Policy for details.
